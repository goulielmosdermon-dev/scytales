How partners put digital identity into production

1st US bank to accept an mDL

America First Credit Union verifies members by cryptographic handshake

Terminal verifiers across every branch accept the Utah Mobile Driving Licence. Encrypted, real-time checks ended spoofing at the teller window and cut verification time — no plastic handled, nothing keyed in.

27 member states, one wallet

Scytáles builds the reference implementation behind the EU Digital Identity Wallet

Selected with Netcompany-Intrasoft to deliver the EUDI Wallet architecture under eIDAS 2.0. It powers the official EU Wallet, enables cross-border verification between member states, and meets GDPR in full.

See the full story

1st ISO-compliant mDL in the US

Utah replaces the plastic driver’s licence with a contactless mobile one

An mDL ecosystem built entirely on ISO/IEC 18013-5. Hold-to-scan keeps the phone in the citizen’s hand, and verification is separated from tracking — the state cannot see where an ID is used.
